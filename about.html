About NSCC

The Nonlinear Systems and Control Conference (NSCC) is organized by the Program Committee of the TCCT Symposium on Nonlinear Systems and Control. It serves as a premier platform for academic exchange, bringing together scholars and engineering professionals from domestic and international communities to explore the field of nonlinear systems and control. Over the years, NSCC has established itself as a significant event in the control systems community, fostering collaboration and innovation among researchers and practitioners alike.

Since its inception in 2016, NSCC has successfully convened nine times in various cities across China. The 10th NSCC is scheduled to take place in Shanghai, China, from August 18-21, 2026. This milestone event promises to be a dynamic gathering of minds dedicated to advancing the frontiers of nonlinear systems and control.
